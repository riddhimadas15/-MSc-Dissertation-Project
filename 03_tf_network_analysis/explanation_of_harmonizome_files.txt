** source: The gene that is being regulated (target gene).
** source_desc: Likely additional information about the source gene (but mostly "na" in this dataset).
** source_id: An identifier for the source gene.
** target: The transcription factor (TF) that regulates the source gene.
** target_desc: Possibly additional information about the transcription factor.
** target_id: An identifier for the transcription factor.
** weight: A score indicating the strength of the interaction (1.000000 suggests a strong or confirmed interaction).
** Here, The transcription factor ARID3A (target) regulates A1BG (sources).
** The weight column suggests that all these interactions have the same strength (1.0), meaning they are likely direct regulatory interactions.

#What This Means
This dataset represents a list of known transcription factors.
These TFs might be used to identify whether a different set of genes (like your DEGs) are transcription factors.
You can use this dataset to check which differentially expressed genes (DEGs) from your analysis are also TFs.

#How It Connects to Your Previous Data
In your gene-TF interaction network, you are trying to identify which DEGs are TFs and which genes they regulate.
You can compare this list with your DEG gene list to extract TFs from DEGs.
